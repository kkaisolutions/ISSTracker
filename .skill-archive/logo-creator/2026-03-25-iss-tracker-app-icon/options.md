# ISS Tracker App Icon Options

Prepared with the `logo-creator` workflow for a technical, live-telemetry-focused iOS app.

## Brand Read

- Product tone: precise, cinematic, orbital, live-data driven
- Avoid: childish cartoon satellites, cluttered starfields, tiny unreadable Earth details
- Best fit for App Store: bold silhouette, high contrast, one focal object, minimal text
- Core palette from app: deep space navy, electric cyan, cool indigo, warm orbital amber

Palette anchor:

- `#050C1D` deep space
- `#0A5B9A` orbital blue
- `#54DBFF` telemetry cyan
- `#8F9CFF` indigo glow
- `#FFA14F` re-entry amber

## 10 Directions

### 1. Orbital Window

Concept:
Round Earth slice viewed through a dark cockpit window with a crisp ISS silhouette crossing the upper edge.

Why it works:
Feels premium and observational. Communicates "live view from orbit" instead of generic space wallpaper.

Visual build:
- deep navy background
- partial Earth horizon with cyan atmosphere glow
- small but distinct ISS silhouette
- thin orbital arc

Prompt:
`Premium iOS app icon for "ISS Tracker", dark space background, partial Earth horizon with cyan atmospheric glow, crisp International Space Station silhouette crossing the upper right, minimal orbital arc, clean vector-style shapes, bold central composition, high contrast, no text, no mockup, designed for App Store icon readability`

### 2. Radar Lock

Concept:
A simplified top-down radar reticle centered on a tiny ISS mark above the Earth.

Why it works:
Leans into tracking and telemetry rather than astronomy. Strong at small sizes.

Visual build:
- circular targeting rings
- one ISS glyph at center
- subtle globe texture behind
- cyan plus amber accent

Prompt:
`Modern tracking-themed app icon for "ISS Tracker", centered radar reticle locking onto a simplified ISS symbol over a dark globe, telemetry cyan lines, small amber accent, minimal vector design, sharp geometric forms, strong silhouette, no text, plain dark background`

### 3. Globe + Flight Path

Concept:
A clean globe with one bright orbital sweep and a single station marker.

Why it works:
Closest to the current icon, but more refined and App-Store-ready.

Visual build:
- spherical Earth
- one luminous orbit ring
- tiny ISS node with glow
- fewer land details than current icon

Prompt:
`Refined app icon for "ISS Tracker", clean blue Earth sphere with one bright orbital path line wrapping around it, minimal landmass hints, small glowing ISS marker, premium flat-plus-glass style, simplified for mobile icon legibility, no text, no mockup`

### 4. Monoline Station Emblem

Concept:
The ISS drawn as a white monoline emblem on a dark square with a faint blue halo.

Why it works:
Most minimal and most distinct. Good if you want a serious utility-tool feel.

Visual build:
- center-aligned ISS glyph
- soft radial glow
- no Earth, no stars
- strict geometry

Prompt:
`Minimalist utility app icon for "ISS Tracker", centered white monoline International Space Station emblem on deep navy background, subtle cyan radial glow, ultra-clean geometric vector style, highly legible at small sizes, no text, no extra scenery`

### 5. Horizon Pass

Concept:
The ISS crossing an illuminated Earth horizon with a dramatic diagonal composition.

Why it works:
More cinematic while still simple enough for an icon.

Visual build:
- dark lower hemisphere
- bright atmospheric arc
- station silhouette at diagonal
- one warm highlight

Prompt:
`Cinematic but minimal iOS app icon for "ISS Tracker", ISS silhouette passing diagonally across a glowing Earth horizon, deep navy space, cyan atmosphere edge, tiny warm sunrise accent, clean simplified shapes, premium icon composition, no text, no clutter`

### 6. Telemetry Badge

Concept:
An abstract badge combining orbit line, dot marker, and grid ticks to imply live telemetry.

Why it works:
Feels like a professional instrument app, not a public museum app.

Visual build:
- dark badge base
- orbital curve
- moving-position dot
- fine grid or latitude marks

Prompt:
`High-tech telemetry app icon for "ISS Tracker", abstract badge design combining orbit arc, position dot, subtle grid ticks, and a compact ISS-inspired central mark, deep space navy with cyan and indigo accents, vector precision, no text, designed for sharp mobile readability`

### 7. Night Earth Crosshair

Concept:
A dark Earth seen from space with one illuminated point and a crosshair offset over it.

Why it works:
Emphasizes the "where is it now" use case.

Visual build:
- mostly dark globe
- one bright tracked point
- crosshair or locator corner marks
- minimal night-light texture

Prompt:
`Tracking-focused app icon for "ISS Tracker", dark Earth from orbit with a single bright live position point and subtle crosshair locator marks, minimal city-light texture, elegant cyan glow, bold simplified composition, no text, no mockup`

### 8. Orbital Compass

Concept:
Compass-like circular instrument face with the ISS as the needle substitute.

Why it works:
Combines navigation and space tracking in one memorable shape.

Visual build:
- instrument dial
- orbit path replacing compass ring
- ISS as pointer element
- cyan/indigo glass finish

Prompt:
`Sophisticated navigation-inspired app icon for "ISS Tracker", circular orbital compass face with the ISS used as the pointer element, telemetry cyan and indigo highlights, dark glassy background, simplified vector interface aesthetic, no text, strong App Store icon readability`

### 9. Footprint Field

Concept:
Earth globe with a translucent circular footprint zone and station marker above it.

Why it works:
Unique to ISS tracking. References the app’s footprint-circle behavior.

Visual build:
- Earth hemisphere
- translucent red-orange coverage circle
- ISS above with path hint
- cyan globe shading

Prompt:
`Distinctive space-tracking app icon for "ISS Tracker", blue Earth hemisphere with a translucent coverage footprint circle and a small ISS marker above it, clean orbital path hint, modern vector style, dark premium background, no text, no mockup`

### 10. Glass Capsule

Concept:
A glossy square icon with a frosted inner panel containing a simplified globe and ISS glyph.

Why it works:
Matches Apple-style premium icon treatment while keeping the symbol system simple.

Visual build:
- dark outer body
- inner frosted panel
- cyan globe arc
- white ISS glyph

Prompt:
`Premium iOS glass-style app icon for "ISS Tracker", dark outer square with frosted inner panel, simplified globe arc and clean white ISS glyph, cyan and indigo glow accents, elegant restrained depth, high contrast, no text, no mockup, App Store ready`

## Recommended Shortlist

Best three to generate first:

1. `Orbital Window` for premium product feel
2. `Radar Lock` for strongest tracking story
3. `Footprint Field` for the most app-specific concept

## Generation Notes

- Generate in `1:1`
- Keep backgrounds simple and dark
- Ask for `high legibility at small sizes`
- Add `no stars unless very subtle`
- Add `no detailed solar panels` if the ISS becomes too busy
- Add `centered composition` if the model drifts into poster layouts
